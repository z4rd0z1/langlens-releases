# LangLens

You see text on your screen that you cannot read. A website, a PDF, a Slack message, a screenshot, a design in Figma. You do not want to copy it, paste it somewhere else, wait, then copy it back.

LangLens sits in the menu bar on a Mac. You press a shortcut, drag a box over the words, and a small bubble shows the translation. Done. Nothing to paste. Nothing saved. You can close it and keep working.

That is the whole idea.

## Why it exists

I got tired of leaving whatever I was looking at just to translate a few lines. LangLens keeps the translation on top of the thing you are already looking at.

## How to use it

1. Download the latest `.dmg` from the [Releases](https://github.com/z4rd0z1/langlens-releases/releases) page on this repo.
2. Open the file and drag LangLens into your Applications folder.
3. Open LangLens. The first time, Mac may ask for Screen Recording permission. Turn it on so LangLens can see the text on your screen.
4. Look for LangLens in the menu bar at the top of the screen (not in the Dock).
5. Press **⌘⇧Space**, or click the menu bar icon and choose Translate.
6. Drag a box over the text. Read the bubble. Press Esc when you are finished.

You do not need an account. You do not need to paste an API key. It should just work.

## What you need

An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later).
